Filtering after retrieval also stops content leaks, but ranking still uses documents the user can't see. It answered a private-incident question with an unrelated doc, and whether it refuses can depend on restricted content. Filtering first removes both problems.

Who can read what

Each connector translates its source's rules (GitHub teams, Slack channel membership, Linear private teams, Notion sharing) into an explicit list of people. Security can audit any document's readers here. Identities are joined on email through the company directory; anything that doesn't match is dropped.
